# 📘 NexusIQ User Guide — Pilot v1.0

## For Managers

### Getting Started

1. **Create Account**
   - Go to: http://your-nexusiq-url.com/auth/register
   - Enter your details
   - Select role: **Manager**
   - Click "Create Account"

2. **Login**
   - Email & password
   - You'll land on the Manager Dashboard

### Creating Your First Course

#### Option 1: New Project Training
Perfect for teaching employees to build something from scratch.

1. Go to **Courses** → Click **New Course**
2. Enter course details:
   - Title: "Build a REST API with FastAPI"
   - Description: "Learn to create production APIs"
   - Mode: **New Project**
3. Click **Continue**
4. Upload your project spec (PDF, DOCX, or TXT)
   - Example: Technical requirements doc, user stories, architecture diagram
5. Click **Generate Course**
6. Wait ~60 seconds while AI creates the curriculum

#### Option 2: Legacy Codebase Training
Perfect for onboarding to existing code.

1. Same setup, but choose mode: **Legacy Codebase**
2. Upload source code files (.py, .js, .java, etc.)
3. AI will analyze and create teaching modules

### Assigning Courses

1. Click on any course in your courses list
2. You'll see all employees
3. Click **Assign** next to each employee name
4. They'll see the course in their dashboard immediately

### Monitoring Progress

- **Dashboard**: See total courses, employees, active learners
- **Course Details**: Click any course to see who's assigned
- Future: Detailed analytics on completion rates, scores, time spent

## For Employees

### Getting Started

1. **Create Account**
   - Register at: http://your-nexusiq-url.com/auth/register
   - Role: **Employee**

2. **Login**
   - You'll see your **Dashboard** with assigned courses

### Learning a Course

1. Click any assigned course
2. You'll enter the **Learning Mode**:
   - Left sidebar: Module list
   - Main area: AI tutor chat

3. **Chat with your AI tutor**:
   - Ask questions: "Can you explain dependency injection?"
   - Request examples: "Show me a code example"
   - Clarify concepts: "I don't understand why we use async here"
   - The tutor adapts to your pace!

4. **Progress**:
   - Top bar shows % of concepts covered
   - Tutor will guide you through all learning goals
   - When teaching is complete, click **Evaluate**

### Evaluation Phase

Evaluation has 2 parts (no cheating — it's AI-proctored!)

#### Part 1: Coding Task (40% of grade)
1. Read the task requirements
2. Write your solution (code file)
3. Upload and submit
4. AI reviews your code instantly
5. Get feedback + score
6. **Must pass** (≥60%) to proceed to interview

#### Part 2: Technical Interview (60% of grade)
1. AI conducts a 9-question interview
2. Questions get progressively harder
3. Type your answers (conversational — explain your thinking!)
4. Takes ~15 minutes

#### Results
- **Pass** (≥60% final): Move to next module 🎉
- **Fail**: Get a personalized tutor plan, retry the module

### Tips for Success

✅ **DO:**
- Ask lots of questions during learning
- Explain your thinking in interviews
- Review feedback carefully if you fail
- Take breaks — AI remembers your progress

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rush through teaching phase
- Copy-paste code without understanding
- Give one-word answers in interviews
- Stress — you can retry modules!

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't see my course"
- Check if your manager assigned it
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R)
- Ask your manager to verify assignment

### "AI tutor isn't responding"
- Check your internet connection
- Wait 10 seconds and try again
- Contact support if it persists

### "My evaluation is stuck"
- For tasks: Make sure you uploaded a file
- For interviews: Type your answer and press Enter
- Refresh if truly stuck (progress is saved)

### "I failed the module"
- Read your tutor plan (shows weak areas)
- Re-do the teaching phase
- Ask more questions this time
- Retry evaluation when ready

## FAQ

**Q: How long does a module take?**
A: Varies by content. Expect 1-2 hours learning + 30 min evaluation.

**Q: Can I pause and resume?**
A: Yes! Your progress auto-saves. Just log back in.

**Q: What if I fail a module?**
A: You get personalized revision guidance and can retry unlimited times.

**Q: Is the interview live with a human?**
A: No — it's an AI interviewer. Totally judgment-free zone!

**Q: Can I skip ahead to harder modules?**
A: No, modules unlock sequentially. This ensures you have prerequisites.

**Q: What happens when I complete all modules?**
A: You get a completion badge! Your manager sees your final scores.
